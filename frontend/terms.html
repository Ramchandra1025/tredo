TERMS FOR USING TRADO

Learn safely

These in-app terms explain the intended use of this learning project.

Educational use

TRADO provides general trading education and practice activities. It is not financial, investment, tax, or legal advice, and it does not recommend buying or selling any asset.

Simulations and rewards

Charts, prices, and paper trades are illustrative or user-entered simulations unless clearly stated otherwise. Virtual coins, XP, lives, and badges have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged.

Account and learning data

You are responsible for keeping your login details private. TRADO stores your account details, preferences, lesson progress, and activity so it can provide the learning features you use.

Use with care

Market examples simplify real conditions. Do not use a lesson or simulation as the sole basis for a financial decision. The project may change as it is developed.
